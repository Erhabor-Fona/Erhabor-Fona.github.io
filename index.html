MemoryMap

Remember where the numbers were. Tap them back in order. Three attempts per run, then we rate you.

How it works

Numbers appear across the board, then vanish. Tap them back in order from memory. Each stage adds another number and takes away a little time. Clear enough stages and you earn a badge named after a thinker, from Curie to Spinoza.

Every run ends with a cognitive score out of 1000, and you can put it on a global or country leaderboard under a name you choose.

Support

Something broken, a score that looks wrong, or a name you want released? Email support.memory.map@gmail.com and describe what happened. If it involves the leaderboard, include the name you play under.

Privacy

Memory Map asks for no account and no personal details. Read the privacy policy for what is stored and how to have it deleted.
